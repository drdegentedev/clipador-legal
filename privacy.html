Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Data we access

With the account owner's authorization, the App reads: basic profile information (account id, display name) and, for videos posted by that account, video id, publish date and aggregate metrics (views, likes, comments, shares). It does not read private messages, followers' personal data, or any data of other users.

How we use it

Metrics are written to an internal spreadsheet used only to evaluate which edits perform better. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens and metrics are stored locally on the operator's computer, outside any public repository. Tokens are read-only.

Retention and deletion

Metrics are kept while the account is operated by us. To request deletion, revoke the App in the platform's settings and email us; we delete stored tokens and metrics within 30 days.

Google / YouTube

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
